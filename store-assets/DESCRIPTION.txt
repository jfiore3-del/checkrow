✅ CheckRow is a to-do list with reminders that lives in Chrome's side panel, right next to whatever you're working on. No account. No sign-in. No servers. Just your list, always one click away.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🗂️ ORGANIZE
• Lists for work, home and everything in between, plus Today, Starred and All tasks views
• Switch lists in one click from the ▾ next to the list name
• Rename a list right where it sits: click the name, type, done
• Due dates and priorities you set as you type and change right on the task
• 🔁 Repeating tasks: daily, every weekday, weekly, monthly or yearly
• 🧩 Sub-tasks: break big tasks into small ones, with a green progress bar that fills in as you go (“60% · 2 sub-tasks left”)

⏰ STAY ON TOP OF IT
• Reminders on any task: once at the exact time you choose, or on a repeating schedule
• Act right from the notification: 😴 Snooze 1 hour or ✔️ Mark complete
• Option to keep reminders on screen until you deal with them
• 📅 Add any task to Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar app (.ics)
• A daily reminder on your work days to plan your day
• A count on the toolbar icon of what's overdue or due today
• ✨ AI Brief: one click turns your list into a morning brief with your AI assistant, or sends it to your team chat

⚡ FAST TO USE
• ⌨️ Alt+Shift+K opens CheckRow from anywhere
• 🖱️ Right-click any text, link or page and choose “Add to CheckRow”
• Long task names expand when you hover, and the ⋮ drawer keeps every option tidy
• Open a full tab for a bigger three-pane view
• Light and dark themes

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Syncs across your computers through your Chrome account, with nothing to sign in to
• Edit on two computers? Changes merge field by field, so nothing gets lost
• 💾 Daily backups and an archive of older completed tasks, kept on your computer
• Export everything or just one list. Import a file as new lists, or restore it all
• 🚫 No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The developer never sees your tasks.
