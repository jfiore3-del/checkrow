# CheckRow Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

CheckRow is a to-do list extension for Google Chrome. It has no servers: the developer never receives, sees, sells or shares your information. This policy explains what CheckRow stores and the only times it sends anything anywhere.

## What CheckRow stores

- Your lists and tasks (titles, notes, due dates, priorities, stars and completion) and your settings (default sort order, theme, and daily reminder days and time).
- These are kept with the browser's extension storage. If you're signed in to your browser with sync turned on, the browser syncs them across your computers through your browser account, under Google's privacy policy. If sync is off, they stay on that computer.
- If you enter a work email in Settings, it is stored only on that computer and is not synced.

## What CheckRow sends, and when

CheckRow sends nothing on its own. Only when you click a button in the AI Brief window:

- Your brief (the task list shown in that window, which you can edit or limit to one list first) is copied to your clipboard, and the service you picked opens in a new tab.
- For Claude, ChatGPT and Grok, the brief is included in the link so the message box can be filled in.
- If you saved a work email, it is added to Microsoft Teams, Google Chat and Gemini links so they open in that account. The Teams link also includes the brief.

Once your text reaches one of those services, that service's privacy policy applies.

## What CheckRow doesn't do

No analytics, ads or tracking. It doesn't read the pages you visit or your browsing history, has no accounts or sign-ins, and runs no code from the internet. The daily reminder is optional and created entirely in your browser.

## Your choices

Delete tasks and lists at any time, or export a backup from the sidebar. Turn the reminder off and clear your work email in Settings. Uninstalling CheckRow deletes the data it stored in that browser.

## Children

CheckRow isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated, and changes to how data is handled will be noted in the extension's release notes.
